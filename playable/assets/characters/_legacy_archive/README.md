# Historical future-character manifests

V14 ships Darya, Setayesh and Uzudi as reference-driven characters under their own folders. Setayesh and Uzudi have been promoted; their obsolete future slots were removed. Retired characters have no art or manifests here and cannot be activated through the V14 roster/API.

The remaining folders describe future reference rebuilds for unshipped characters (amirreza, parsa, iman, mojtaba, hossein, arsal, sorkhi, taher-db). They are development records, not playable roster entries. A future character needs supplied references, complete poses, the shared V14 atlas/motion contract and passing visual/runtime QA before installation.

Current standard: `demian/docs/character-standards/MOBILE_CHARACTER_V14.fa.md`.
