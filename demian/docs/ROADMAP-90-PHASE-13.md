# Phase 13 Café Drift prize readiness

Café Drift prize eligibility requires ranked replay, the verified-phone and OTP rollout, anti-cheat, legal acknowledgment and publication approval, weekly settlement, approved campaign budgets, and in-cafe coupon issuance. The readiness check binds pilot evidence to the rules version and SHA-256 digests of the current controls, rendering, replay and reward decision sources. The protected Laravel package includes the same four frontend sources used in that check.

Approval requires a reviewed independent pilot of at least 20 participants. Record physical Android, iOS and desktop checks, replay and identity findings, cashier redemption, and the owner's budget evidence in `cafe-drift-prize-acceptance.json`. The effective date is a Tehran Saturday following the review. The approval lasts up to 90 days from the review as long as the source digests remain current. Prize eligibility is frozen with the weekly rules and rechecked before allocation and issuance. The checked-in pilot is pending.

Shared or identical replay evidence remains available to the superadmin as context for corroboration. Emulator checks compare the same steering targets across six viewports. The replay suite compares server outcomes with 96 additional deterministic browser runs.
