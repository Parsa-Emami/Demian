# Phase 11 experience and loyalty

The server grants 100 XP for a qualifying settled week, up to 50 XP for playing distinct additional games, 50 XP for a personal breakthrough and 25 XP for completing the immediately preceding week. The base grant is capped at 225 XP. A late historical settlement grants participation and variety. Phase 12 contributes up to 75 additional mission XP per week.

Lifetime XP determines one level per 250 XP and five loyalty tiers. The private `GET /api/v1/arcade/player/loyalty` view shows the lifetime total and the last 12 grants. Each settled grant stores its XP components and weekly source digest. The player's data export includes the grants and account erasure removes them. Loyalty grants confer progress levels and have a financial budget of zero.
