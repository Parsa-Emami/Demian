# Roadmap 90+ — Phase 03: coupon economics and campaign budget

Implemented on an independent branch; GitHub Actions acceptance and sequential merge after phases 1/2 remain pending. No live host, financial flags, secrets or real money changed.

## Financial contract
- One weekly campaign per Tehran period across all prize games; the existing SQL unique constraint, period/campaign locks and atomic issuance ensure one budget. Owner sets DEMIAN_COUPONS_WEEKLY_BUDGET_MINOR (integer in the approved currency's minor unit; no default). Future campaigns require superadmin confirmation, full economics plan and existing inventory coverage. In testing, old fixtures can still exercise the historic path, or set DEMIAN_COUPONS_ECONOMICS_REQUIRED=true to enforce the new gate.
- Freeze budget, capacity, discount tiers, minimum bill, per-voucher cap, cost ceiling, after-discount contribution-margin floor, assumed incremental share and planned operating cost in the existing terms hash. No migration/backfill; old frozen terms hashes remain valid. New live weekly issuance without economics/campaign is blocked. Already-issued historical obligations are not silently re-priced.
- Worst-case commitment = max_awards × per-coupon cap. Issued liability is not recycled after redemption, expiry or revocation; it cannot be double-allocated. Each issue batch revalidates commitments and current owner ceiling. Redemption keeps actual discount formula and checks frozen approved cost ceiling × gross and minimum contribution margin × net sales using integer rounding. Cashier cannot lower announced discount at checkout. A breach blocks the transaction.
- Suggested draft rates 20/15/12/8 percent, still under existing approved maxima. Break-even screening at full redemption and minimum bill uses highest tier for every award; this is a specified scenario, not proof of profit, and actual economics can differ. Cost ceiling and incrementality assumptions must come from actual accounting; no hard-coded business rates. Pilot and menu/POS cost validation required. The live readiness check requires the owner ceiling when weekly issuance or campaigns are enabled. Both cPanel example environments leave it blank. Actual cost per basket is not automatically ingested.
- Weekly competition discount budget is distinct from gifts; manual gifts retain existing separately approved issuance gate and limits, not silently counted as weekly prize expense. A separate gift budget or unified paid marketing budget is needed before enabling gifts.

## Reporting
Planned worst-case maximum liability and minimum-basket full-redemption scenario are displayed separately from actual ledger totals. Actual POS coupon ledger sales before/after discount, redeemed count/rate, cumulative issued cap, outstanding unexpired liability, remaining issuance capacity in money, modeled variable costs and net incremental contribution. Estimated incremental contribution = gross × (1 - cost ceiling) × assumed incremental share. ROI = (incremental contribution before coupon costs - actual discount - operating cost) / (discount + operating cost). Zero denominator is undefined. No automatic claim that a coupon sale is incremental.
After weekly settlement, closure and resolution of all vouchers, superadmin can submit audited signed incremental contribution and actual operating cost with a non-PII accounting/control-study reference. Existing append-only event log snapshots ledger totals, records actor and date; corrections append events. A changed ledger invalidates the displayed measurement. This is operator-attested evidence, not independently verified POS attribution.
