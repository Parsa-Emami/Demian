# Roadmap 90+ — Phase 04: dynamic winner allocation

Depends on Phase 03 economics (stacked PR). No real hosting or financial activation.
New funded campaigns freeze dynamic-first-time-v1 inside the existing terms hash before the week. Legacy campaign terms remain immutable; test fixtures can opt into the new policy via demian-coupons.dynamic_allocation_required. New non-test weeks with no sponsor preserve technical ranks without financial winners. No schema migration.

- Unique verified participants per game; threshold 20, target floor(15% × participants), bounded by eligible accounts and the per-game cap: Dino Run 10, Café Drift 10, Coffee Crash 8, Café Rush 8, Café Critters 5, After Hours 5, Open World 4. Only currently approved prizes and frozen season eligibility count; the latter three remain locked out until their separate approvals. Existing 1–10 discount tier contract remains intact.
- Capacity is capped by max_awards and floor(frozen budget / per-voucher maximum). At least one seat per qualified game when funds permit, remainder deterministic highest averages. Cross-game augmenting matching favors never-awarded accounts then technical rank, not raw cross-game score; past winners can fill remaining seats, active weekly voucher holders excluded. Unused overlapping seats can move within targets. Underfunded weeks never issue partial unapproved liabilities.
- Same engine for management preview and final settlement. Preview uses verified bests only, distinct accounts, no individual list or write; provisional until closing. Final settlement reuses existing trust checks, transaction and locks; if qualifying awards exist but campaign inactive or stock short, refuse to seal, not silently reduce awards. Persist aggregate per-game quotas, rates, frozen terms hash and separate canonical winner hash in existing sealed event. Issuance verifies this hash before generating money. Technical rankings unchanged. Rates are observed ratios, not random probabilities.
- Unit boundary/deduplication/budget/determinism tests, feature settlement/issue/tamper tests. Real participant identity and bot prevention are subsequent phases. A person with multiple accounts remains possible until verified identity rollout.
